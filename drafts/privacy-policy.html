Who We Are

Northstar Digital LLC ("Northstar Digital," "we," "us") provides AI receptionist, lead-handling, websites, and marketing systems for businesses in West Virginia. Contact: business@northstardigitalmarketing.info · (304) 484-4648 · (304) 381-1611.

This policy describes how we handle personal information in connection with our website, inquiries, and services. It should be read with our Terms of Service. When we provide tools for a business client, that client may independently determine what information is collected from their customers; that client's own privacy notice may also apply.

Information We May Process

  • Contact information, business details, and information submitted through forms or conversations (name, business name, phone, email, message content).
  • Appointment requests, service inquiries, and CRM records.
  • Communications such as emails, SMS messages, chatbot exchanges, and call information, including recordings or transcripts when enabled and permitted.
  • Website usage and technical information when analytics or similar tools are enabled.
  • Payment and billing details handled through payment service providers.

Not every category is collected in every interaction. We do not sell your personal information. We do not buy, rent, or use purchased phone-number lists for our own SMS program.

How Information Is Used

  • Respond to inquiries, schedule appointments, and provide requested services.
  • Operate and troubleshoot websites, CRM systems, AI receptionists, chatbots, and follow-up workflows.
  • Maintain business records, support customers, prevent abuse, and meet applicable obligations.
  • Send service-related communications and, where you have opted in, marketing communications.

SMS / Text Messaging

If you provide your mobile number and check a consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive text messages from Northstar Digital LLC. Two separate, optional consent options are offered:

  • Account & service texts — appointment scheduling, account updates, and information related to your audit or project request.
  • Marketing texts — special offers, discounts, and service updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

No Sharing of Mobile Information With Third Parties

Mobile opt-in and consent information, and the phone numbers collected for SMS, are never shared, sold, or rented to any third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties except as required to deliver the messaging service itself (for example, our messaging provider), and those providers are prohibited from using the information for any other purpose.

AI, CRM, and Service Providers

Our services may use GoHighLevel, communications providers, hosting providers, payment processors, and other technology vendors. Information may be processed by these providers to deliver the requested functionality. Their own terms and privacy practices may apply.

That processor use does not authorize sharing SMS opt-in data for a third party's own marketing. We do not make unverified claims about whether any provider uses information for model training.

Calls, Texts, Recordings, and Consent

AI-powered calls, recordings, automated messages, and follow-ups may be subject to consent, disclosure, and marketing rules. Recording and automation features should only be enabled with appropriate notices and permissions. Business clients are responsible for the lawful collection of their customers' information and the contact lists, permissions, and messages they direct us to use.

Sharing and Protection

Information may be shared with service providers, authorized client personnel, and other parties when needed to operate the service, comply with law, or protect rights and security. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and Requests

Information is retained as needed for service delivery, business records, legal obligations, and legitimate operational purposes. Actual retention periods depend on the service, client settings, and provider systems.

To request access, correction, or deletion of information held by Northstar, contact us using the details below. Some requests may need to be directed to the business client that controls the information.

Policy Changes

We may update this policy as services and practices change. The date at the top identifies this version.
